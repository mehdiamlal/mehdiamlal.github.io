I am a PhD researcher at the [University of Bologna](https://cvlab.unibo.it) and AMD Silo AI, as part of the World Model Foundry. I am advised by [Matteo Poggi](https://mattpoggi.github.io) and [Fabio Tosi](https://fabiotosi92.github.io) at UniBO, and by [Pier Luigi Dovesi](https://dovesi.dev) at AMD.

I am also a researcher and board member of [The Good AI Lab](https://thegoodailab.org), an independent lab doing foundational AI research for social good, where I coordinate our partnerships with universities and academic researchers.

My research is on Physical AI and foundation models for robotics, with a focus on 3D perception and world models: how an agent can recover the geometry of the scene around it, and use that understanding to anticipate how the world will change as it acts.

Previously, I worked on domain adaptation, weight-space learning, and model merging.

I enjoy learning from first principles: taking complex systems apart down to their elementary components, then rebuilding them from scratch to understand why they work.
